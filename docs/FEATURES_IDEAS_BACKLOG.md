# Features Ideas Backlog (Expanding Scope)

Grouped by domain. Use as a source for roadmap candidates.

## Visual Enrichment & Editing
- Smart lower-third detector; auto-label speaker names from OCR + diarization.
- Logo/product placement detection; brand safety scoring per segment.
- Object/person tracking with ID persistence; region-based blur/redact.
- Provider hub tasks: bg removal, inpaint/outpaint, upscale, stylize; video masking/inpaint.
- Auto thumbnails & B‑roll generation from prompts/context.

## Search & Retrieval
- Temporal query builder: “Find segments where <entity> appears within 10s of <keyword>”.
- Cross-modal RAG (transcripts + OCR + visual tags) with time-aligned citations.
- Dynamic facets (topics/people/brands) with timelines and density plots.

## Education & Productivity
- Study packs: flashcards/quizzes (Anki/Notion export), spaced repetition schedules.
- Lecture outline autogeneration; concept maps; slide/whiteboard OCR.
- Rubric-based grading assistant; feedback generator for educators.

## Live/SSAI & Distribution
- Live captions/translation with confidence overlays; DVR search on live streams.
- SSAI metadata generation; ad-safe region tagging; sponsor detection.
- Social clip packs (title/desc/hashtags), A/B thumbnail testing.

## Pro Media Supply Chain
- IMF/MXF parsers; deliverable validation (AS-11); sidecar management.
- Proxy conform/relink; EDL/AAF/XML roundtrip; timeline compare.
- QC pack: loudness, flash/black/freeze/PSE, VMAF/SSIM/PSNR.

## Rights, Licensing, Monetization
- Rights catalog (territory/window/exclusivity/embargo); conflict detection.
- Usage metering; revenue share; takedown orchestration.
- License templates; negotiation workflows; watermarking for screeners.

## Security, Privacy, Compliance
- PII/PHI detection/redaction for text/audio/video; consent registry.
- Data residency policies; tenant isolation; immutable audit trails.
- DSR workflows; continuous compliance evidence; policy-as-code.

## Developer Experience
- SDKs for Web/Node/Python/Swift/Kotlin; codegen pipeline.
- Webhooks catalog; event replay; mock servers; contract testing.
- Partner portal with usage analytics, quotas, monetization tiers.

## Operations & FinOps
- Cost dashboards per tenant/workflow; preflight cost simulations.
- Auto-scaling GPU pools; job queue SLOs; anomaly detection on failures/cost.
- Energy/green compute metrics; off-peak scheduling policies.

## Collaboration & Review
- Timeline comments, mentions, approvals; version compare.
- Watermarked review links (forensic id), viewer analytics.
- Annotation layers; export annotations to NLE/JSON.

## Personalization & Recommendations
- Hybrid recommenders (content + collaborative); session-based ranking.
- Diversity/novelty controls; fairness metrics; A/B testing harness.

## Localization & Accessibility
- Caption authoring QC; SDH features; reading speed checks.
- Audio description authoring; sign-language track support.
- Translation memory and glossary; LQA dashboards.
